How the build went

Steps and wording from the shop's own post on this Escalade.

1

Polish the paint first

The heavily contaminated paint gets polished so the vinyl wrap can stick to a clean, smooth surface.

2

Satin black wrap

Full disassembly of the vehicle, plus a new chrome grill.

3

Lights and tint

LED headlights, park lights and fog lights. Window tint at 5% front, 5% back and 50% windshield.

4

Lowered, on 26s

Once it was wrapped, a 2/4 Belltech lowering kit and 26 inch wheels.

5

Brakes and exhaust

Drilled and slotted rotors with bright orange caliper paint. A custom dual side exit exhaust for a little more rumble and a nostalgic old school look.

Head-turner
