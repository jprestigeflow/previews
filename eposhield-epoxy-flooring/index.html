We don’t coat over stains. We diamond grind the top of the slab off first, so the coating grips clean concrete. Eposhield, on this Oakville garage.

What happens to your slab.

Eposhield shows its prep work on Instagram. Here is the order they work in.

1

Diamond grind

Takes the stains and the top of the slab off, so the coating grips clean concrete.

From their Oakville and Etobicoke posts
2

Fill the cracks

Coat over a crack and it shows right back through. So they grind it open, pour in a two-part filler and scrape it flush.

From their Burlington crack post
3

Bury the black base

Flake only sticks while the base is wet, so they keep throwing until no black shows. No thin spots, no bald patches.

From their flake post
4

Seal it clear

Loose flake gets scraped off, then the floor is sealed with a clear top coat that wipes clean. Their Etobicoke garage used polyaspartic.
